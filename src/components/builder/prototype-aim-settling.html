Question (#369): while you place, draw and drag, should mirrors re-aim continuously, only when you let go, or only when asked, and does a rough, half-laid-out table behave sensibly? Rules on trial: a mirror's angle comes from the positions of the Component before it and the one it aims at (n ∝ d_out − d_in); the one before it is worked out (whatever aims at it, else whatever light reaches it from, first in list on a tie); when it can't be worked out the mirror keeps its last angle and shows a hollow marker. Drawing a Beam in Beam mode sets the aims; so does dragging the knob on a selected mirror's ring onto a Component. A mirror in the light with no aim sits at 45° to the light: F (or the mouse wheel) flips it to the other side. Mirrors re-aim continuously. The traced beam (red) is only a check.
